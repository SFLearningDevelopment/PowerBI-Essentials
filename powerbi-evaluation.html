📋 Exam Rules

  • Sampling: Each attempt draws 40 questions from a bank of 154, weighted by module exam-weight. No two attempts see identical questions.

  • Attempts: 3 maximum. Highest score is recorded. Pass on any attempt and the certification is yours.
